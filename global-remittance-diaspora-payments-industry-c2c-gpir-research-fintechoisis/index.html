Global Remittance & Diaspora Payments Industry (C2C) | GPIR Research | FINTECHOISIS

Remittances represent the movement of funds by individuals across international borders for personal, family, educational, healthcare and small-business support purposes. Unlike trade payments, which are driven by commerce, remittance flows are primarily driven by migration and diaspora communities.
