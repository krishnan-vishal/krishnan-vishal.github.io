    1. Purpose of This Policy

    This Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy ("IP Policy") explains the ownership, protection, permitted use, attribution and licensing of intellectual property associated with Fintechoisis and the Global Payments Intelligence Repository (GPIR).

    Fintechoisis is being developed as an independent research and intelligence platform documenting and analysing the evolution of payments, banking infrastructure, financial-market infrastructure, fintech, remittances, financial technology, regulation, digital assets and the wider financial-services ecosystem.

    The Repository is intended to evolve into a structured intelligence platform incorporating research, datasets, country intelligence, regional intelligence, analytical frameworks, visualisations, research tools and technology-assisted discovery.

    This Policy should be read together with the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy applicable to the platform.

    4. Original Research and Analysis

    GPIR may combine information from multiple sources to create original research and analytical outputs, for example: source material → data validation → classification → normalisation → analysis → interpretation → research output.

    The underlying facts may be publicly available; however, the original selection, organisation, compilation, classification, methodology, interpretation, presentation and visualisation may constitute original intellectual property.

    Users must distinguish between the underlying public information and GPIR's original research expression or compilation.

    5. Public Facts Versus Original Expression

    GPIR does not claim exclusive ownership over facts that are legitimately in the public domain. For example, GPIR does not claim ownership of a central bank's published interest-rate decision; a regulator's publicly issued rule; a company's publicly disclosed transaction volume; a publicly announced payment-system launch; a government-published statistic; or other facts that are not themselves protected by copyright.

    However, the original presentation, analysis, compilation, classification, visualisation or interpretation of such information may be protected. Users should therefore distinguish between a public fact and GPIR's original research expression or compilation of that fact.

    6. Third-Party Content

    The Repository may reference or link to material belonging to third parties, including regulatory publications, central-bank publications, government documents, company reports, academic research, industry reports, public datasets, research papers, news publications, photographs, logos, trademarks, charts, graphics, and other third-party material.

    Ownership of third-party material remains with the relevant rights holder. GPIR does not claim ownership of third-party intellectual property merely because it is referenced, cited, linked to or analysed within the Repository.

    7. Sources and Attribution

    GPIR seeks to identify and attribute material sources where reasonably practicable. Where research relies upon third-party information, the Repository may provide source names, publication titles, publication dates, source links, regulatory references, dataset references, or other appropriate attribution.

    Users who reproduce or rely upon third-party material are responsible for complying with the applicable rights and licensing conditions of the original source. Attribution to GPIR does not replace attribution requirements applicable to the original source.

    8. Copyright in the Website

    The design and structure of Fintechoisis may contain original intellectual property, including page layouts, navigation structures, visual design, typography, content hierarchy, user-interface components, research presentation formats, dashboard structures, information architecture, original graphics, branding elements, interactive research components, and other original design elements.

    Unauthorised copying or reproduction of the website or substantial portions of its design and structure is prohibited to the extent permitted by applicable law.

    9. Research Taxonomy and Knowledge Architecture

    One of the principal intellectual assets of GPIR is its structured approach to organising financial-services knowledge. The Repository may develop proprietary or original classifications covering payments, banking, payment infrastructure, financial market infrastructure, cross-border money movement, remittances, fintech, digital assets, regulation, payment rails, financial institutions, countries, regions, corridors, use cases, technologies, and emerging financial models.

    The underlying concepts may be common industry concepts; however, the particular taxonomy, hierarchy, relationships, classifications and research architecture developed by GPIR may constitute protected intellectual property.

    GPIR may create or maintain datasets containing information collected from multiple sources. A dataset may contain public facts, licensed data, derived data, calculated values, normalised data, categorised information, historical information, research estimates, or original analytical fields.

    The individual underlying facts may not necessarily be copyright-protected. However, the selection, arrangement, structure, compilation, classification, methodology and presentation of a dataset may be protected by applicable copyright, database rights or other intellectual-property laws. Specific datasets may be subject to separate licences or usage restrictions.

    Subject to these Terms, users may use reasonable portions of GPIR material for personal research, academic research, educational purposes, internal business analysis, professional presentations, commentary, criticism, review, and other lawful purposes permitted under applicable copyright law.

    Where material is used, appropriate attribution should be provided. A suggested attribution is: Source: Global Payments Intelligence Repository (GPIR), Fintechoisis — [Research Title], [Year]. Where applicable, the original third-party source should also be acknowledged.

Commercial use may include reselling research; incorporating substantial Repository content into commercial reports; creating a commercial database; licensing GPIR datasets; reproducing substantial research within paid products; incorporating GPIR content into commercial intelligence platforms; using substantial GPIR material in client deliverables; creating competing research products; or commercially redistributing Repository datasets or visualisations.

    These restrictions apply subject to rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

Unless expressly authorised by a separate licence, users must not systematically scrape GPIR content; crawl protected areas; extract substantial datasets; replicate Repository taxonomy; build a competing knowledge graph; use automated extraction to reproduce Repository content; or use substantial GPIR proprietary content to develop a competing commercial intelligence database or service.

Where software or documentation is released under an explicit open-source licence, that licence governs the relevant material — for example, permitting code inspection, modification, forking, redistribution, or commercial use, subject to the conditions of the applicable licence.

Each category of material should be considered under its applicable licence or rights statement.
