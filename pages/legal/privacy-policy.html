    1. Introduction

    Welcome to FINTECHOISIS ("Payments Repository", "we", "us", or "our"). Payments Repository is an independent intelligence, research, knowledge and information platform covering the broad financial-services ecosystem, including but not limited to payments, banking, cross-border payments, remittances, fintech, financial infrastructure, digital financial services, payment networks, financial technology, regulatory developments, digital assets, and related financial-services markets.

    Our website is intended to provide readers, researchers, professionals, institutions, students, analysts and other interested parties with access to research, analysis, datasets, publications, articles, market intelligence, charts, visualisations, references and other informational content.

    This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, protect and disclose information when you access or use our website, subscribe to our publications, contact us, participate in research activities, or otherwise interact with our services.

    By accessing or using the website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

    2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

    This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through:

    • Our website and associated webpages;
    • Online research repositories and databases;
    • Contact and enquiry forms;
    • Newsletter and publication subscriptions;
    • Research, survey or feedback forms;
    • Downloads, registrations and gated content;
    • Communications between you and Payments Repository;
    • Cookies and similar technologies;
    • Any other digital service or platform that expressly links to this Privacy Policy.

    This Privacy Policy does not necessarily apply to websites, applications, products or services operated by third parties that may be linked from our website. Where you access a third-party website through a link provided on our website, that third party's own privacy policy will apply.

    3. Nature of the Platform

    Payments Repository is primarily an information, research and intelligence platform. The content available through the website may include information obtained from publicly available sources, regulatory publications, central banks, financial institutions, industry bodies, academic publications, research organisations, market participants, licensed databases and other sources.

    We seek to present information responsibly and accurately; however, information may change over time and may contain estimates, assumptions, historical information, projections or interpretations.

    Payments Repository does not provide:

    • Banking services;
    • Money-transfer services;
    • Payment-processing services;
    • Investment management;
    • Financial advisory services;
    • Legal advice;
    • Tax advice;
    • Personalised investment recommendations;
    • Credit or lending services; or
    • Any other regulated financial service unless expressly stated otherwise.

    Readers should independently verify information before relying on it for financial, commercial, investment, regulatory, legal or other material decisions.

    • Submit a research enquiry;
    • Participate in surveys;
    • Provide feedback;

    • Communicate with us by email or through other channels.

    • Business email address;
    • Telephone number;
    • Country or region;
    • Areas of professional or research interest;

    • Other information you voluntarily provide.

    Where applicable data-protection laws require a legal basis for processing personal information, we may rely on one or more of the following:

    • Other lawful grounds permitted under applicable data-protection legislation.

    • Survey responses;

Where reasonably possible, information used for public research will be aggregated, anonymised or otherwise presented so that individual contributors are not unnecessarily identified.

    We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:

    • Prevent unlawful activity; or

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will seek to implement appropriate safeguards, which may include contractual protections, recognised transfer mechanisms or other legally permitted measures.

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against:

    • Unauthorised access;

    We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

    • Providing requested services;
    • Maintaining business and communication records;
    • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations;
    • Resolving disputes;
    • Enforcing agreements; and
    • Protecting our legitimate business interests.

    Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the purpose for which it was collected. When personal information is no longer required, we may securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable law.

    • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
    • Request deletion of personal information;
    • Request restriction of certain processing;
    • Object to certain processing;
    • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
    • Request portability of certain information; and
    • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

    These rights are subject to applicable legal limitations and exceptions. To exercise a privacy right, please contact us using the details provided in Section 24 of this Privacy Policy.

    Where you subscribe to a newsletter, publication or other communication from Payments Repository, we may use your contact information to provide those communications. You may unsubscribe at any time by:

    • Using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the communication; or

    Unsubscribing from marketing communications does not necessarily stop operational or transactional communications that are necessary for services you have requested.

Our website is not knowingly directed toward children below the minimum age required under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Our handling of such signals will depend on applicable law, the technologies used on our website and the capabilities of our service providers. Where legally required, we will honour applicable privacy preference signals.
