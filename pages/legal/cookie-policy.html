    1. Introduction

    This Cookie Policy explains how Fintechoisis and the Global Payments Intelligence Repository (GPIR) ("GPIR", "we", "us", or "our") use cookies and similar technologies when you visit or interact with our website.

    Fintechoisis is an independent research and intelligence platform focused on the global financial-services ecosystem, including payments, banking, cross-border money movement, remittances, fintech, financial infrastructure, regulatory developments, digital assets and emerging financial technologies.

    As the platform evolves, cookies and similar technologies may help us understand how users interact with research, datasets, intelligence tools and other resources available through the Repository.

    This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

    2. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that may be placed on your computer, mobile device or other internet-enabled device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a website to recognise a device or remember certain information about a previous visit.

    Similar technologies may include:

    • Web beacons;
    • Pixels;
    • Tags;
    • Local storage;
    • Session identifiers;
    • Device identifiers; and
    • Other technologies that perform functions similar to cookies.

    For simplicity, this Policy refers to all such technologies as "Cookies".

    3. Why We Use Cookies

    Fintechoisis may use Cookies for several purposes, including to:

    • Operate the website;
    • Maintain security;
    • Remember user preferences;
    • Improve website functionality;
    • Understand how visitors use the Repository;
    • Measure research-content engagement;
    • Analyse website performance;
    • Improve navigation;
    • Understand which research areas receive interest;
    • Improve future intelligence tools;
    • Detect technical problems;
    • Support website administration; and
    • Develop and improve the overall user experience.

    We may also use aggregated information derived from Cookies to understand broader patterns of interaction with the Repository.

    4. Categories of Cookies

    Cookies used by Fintechoisis may generally fall into the following categories.

    4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

    These Cookies are required for essential website functions. They may support website navigation, security, session management, basic functionality, access controls, load balancing, and other essential technical functions.

    Because these Cookies may be necessary for the website to operate, they generally cannot be disabled through our cookie-management tools.

    4.2 Functional Cookies

    Functional Cookies allow the website to remember information that improves the user experience — for example, language preferences, display preferences, research filters, user interface settings, previously selected options, or other preferences.

    If these Cookies are disabled, certain personalised functionality may not operate as intended.

    4.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

    Analytics Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with Fintechoisis, providing information such as number of visitors, pages viewed, research areas accessed, time spent on pages, navigation patterns, referral sources, device type, browser type, approximate geographic location, and website performance.

    Analytics information is generally analysed in aggregated or statistical form. The purpose is to understand how the Repository is being used and to improve the platform.

    4.4 Research and Intelligence Usage Cookies

    As GPIR develops its research and intelligence capabilities, certain Cookies or similar technologies may be used to understand how users interact with research tools, country intelligence, regional intelligence, data visualisations, search functionality, research dashboards, interactive datasets, regulatory intelligence, historical timelines, and other analytical resources.

    This information may help us understand which functionality is useful and where the platform can be improved. Where reasonably possible, such information will be analysed in aggregated or pseudonymised form.

    4.5 Marketing and Communication Cookies

    If Fintechoisis introduces newsletters, commercial research, subscriptions, advertising or other communication services, certain Cookies may be used to measure engagement or support communications. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential marketing Cookies.

    4.6 Third-Party Cookies

    Certain third-party service providers may place Cookies or use similar technologies when their services are integrated into our website — for example, providers supporting website analytics, content delivery, security, video or media, email subscriptions, forms, website performance, research tools, cloud infrastructure, or other website functionality.

    Third-party Cookies are controlled by the relevant third party and may be subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

    5. Cookies We May Use

    The specific Cookies used by Fintechoisis may change as the website develops. Depending on the technology stack, Cookies may include services such as:

    CategoryPotential Purpose
    EssentialWebsite functionality and security
    FunctionalRemembering preferences
    AnalyticsUnderstanding website usage
    PerformanceMonitoring website performance
    ResearchMeasuring engagement with research tools
    CommunicationNewsletter and content engagement
    SecurityFraud and abuse prevention
    Third-party servicesSupporting embedded or external functionality

    The actual Cookies deployed on the live website should be reviewed periodically against this Policy.

    6. Cookie Consent

    Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential Cookies, Fintechoisis will provide an appropriate mechanism allowing visitors to accept Cookies, reject non-essential Cookies, select Cookie categories, or change previously provided preferences.

    Strictly necessary Cookies may continue to operate where they are required for the website to function.

    7. Managing Cookie Preferences

    You may manage Cookies in several ways.

    Through Our Website

    Where a Cookie Preference Centre is available, you may use it to accept optional Cookies, reject optional Cookies, select individual categories, or change your preferences.

    Through Your Browser

    Most modern browsers allow users to block Cookies, delete Cookies, block third-party Cookies, or receive notifications when Cookies are being placed. The exact process varies by browser.

    8. Impact of Disabling Cookies

    If you disable or reject Cookies, certain website functionality may be affected — for example, preferences may not be remembered, interactive tools may not function correctly, certain research features may operate differently, website performance may be affected, or some services may become unavailable.

    Strictly necessary Cookies may still be used where required for essential website functionality.

    9. Analytics

    Fintechoisis may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors use the website, helping us understand which research topics are most frequently accessed; which countries or regions generate interest; which pages receive engagement; how visitors navigate the Repository; which tools are used; how users access the website; and whether technical problems affect the user experience.

    Analytics information is primarily used to improve the Repository rather than to identify individual visitors. Where possible, analytics information will be aggregated, anonymised or pseudonymised.

    10. Google Analytics and Similar Services

    If services such as Google Analytics or similar analytics technologies are deployed, they may collect information regarding website usage in accordance with their respective technologies and privacy practices.

    Where required, Fintechoisis will configure such services in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and provide appropriate consent controls. The specific third-party analytics providers used on the live website should be reflected in the Cookie Preference Centre and, where appropriate, in the Privacy Policy.

    11. IP Addresses and Technical Information

    Cookies and related technologies may operate alongside technical information such as IP address, browser information, device information, operating system, referring URL, time and date of access, and technical identifiers.

    Such information may be used for security, analytics, website administration, performance monitoring, fraud prevention, and understanding website usage. IP addresses may be treated as personal information where required by applicable law.

    12. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

    Fintechoisis may use technologies such as browser local storage or session storage where necessary to provide website functionality — for example, to remember preferences, store temporary session information, improve application performance, or support interactive research tools.

    Such technologies will be treated consistently with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

    13. Cookies and Personal Information

    Cookies do not necessarily identify you by name. However, certain Cookie identifiers, IP addresses, device identifiers or related information may constitute personal information under applicable law.

    Where Cookie information constitutes personal information, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

    14. International Data Processing

    Some Cookie providers, analytics services or technology providers may process information outside the country from which you access Fintechoisis. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will seek to use appropriate safeguards.

    15. Data Retention

    Cookie information may be retained for different periods depending on the type and purpose of the Cookie.

    Session Cookies

    These generally expire when the browser session ends.

    Persistent Cookies

    These remain on a device for a defined period or until manually deleted. Retention periods may vary depending on the provider, purpose and technical implementation.

    16. Third-Party Websites

    Fintechoisis may contain links to third-party websites. When you leave our website, the third party may use its own Cookies and similar technologies. We do not control third-party Cookie practices. You should review the relevant third party's privacy and Cookie policies before interacting with those websites.

    17. Future Intelligence Tools and Automated Technologies

    As GPIR evolves, the platform may introduce technology-assisted research capabilities, including automated search, research retrieval, intelligence dashboards, data exploration, AI-assisted research, personalised research interfaces, alerts, notifications, knowledge graphs, and other analytical tools.

    Cookies or similar technologies may be used to remember preferences, maintain sessions or understand interaction with such functionality. Any significant change in the manner in which personal information is processed will be addressed through appropriate updates to our Privacy Policy and other applicable notices.

    18. Children's Privacy

    Fintechoisis is designed primarily for professionals, researchers, academics, students and individuals interested in financial-services research. We do not knowingly use Cookies to intentionally collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

    19. Do Not Track and Privacy Signals

    Some browsers and devices provide privacy-related signals, including "Do Not Track" or similar settings. The treatment of such signals may depend on applicable law, browser capabilities and the technologies deployed on the website. Where legally required, Fintechoisis will honour applicable recognised privacy signals.

    20. Changes to This Cookie Policy

    The technologies used by Fintechoisis may change as the Repository develops. We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect new technologies, new analytics providers, new website functionality, changes in law, new research tools, changes in our operating model, or changes in third-party services.

    The updated Policy will be published on the website with a revised "Last Updated" date.

    21. Relationship With the Privacy Policy

    This Cookie Policy explains the use of Cookies and similar technologies. The Privacy Policy explains more broadly how personal information is collected, processed, stored, used and disclosed. Where information collected through Cookies constitutes personal information, the Privacy Policy will also apply.

    23. Cookie Preference Statement

    Where a Cookie Preference Centre is available, users may access and modify their preferences at any time.

    Your choices regarding optional Cookies will not affect your ability to access the core publicly available research content of Fintechoisis, except where a particular Cookie is technically required for a specific functionality.

    24. Final Statement

    Fintechoisis and GPIR use Cookies primarily to support the operation, security, performance and continuous development of the Repository. As GPIR evolves from a structured research repository into a broader global financial-services intelligence platform, the technologies used to support research discovery, analytics and user experience may also evolve.

    We are committed to maintaining transparency regarding those technologies and to handling information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.
