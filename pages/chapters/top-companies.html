Overview

This directory consolidates named industry participants referenced throughout the repository into a single classification framework, organised by tier and category. Collectively, these institutions facilitate over USD 130–220 trillion in annual cross-border value transfer globally.

Chapter Conclusion

The future cross-border payments industry will be less defined by individual institutions and more by ecosystems. The winning organisations will not necessarily be the largest processors of transactions, but rather those that control liquidity, connectivity, identity, compliance, treasury, AI and payment orchestration across multiple rails and geographies.
