    Global Healthcare, Medical Tourism & Cross-Border Health Payments

    Patients increasingly travel internationally for treatment, while telemedicine and international insurance claims expand the digital side of a fast-growing cross-border payments category.

    Executive Summary

    Healthcare is rapidly becoming one of the most significant categories within cross-border payments. Patients increasingly travel internationally for elective surgery, oncology, fertility treatment, organ transplantation and cosmetic procedures, while telemedicine, cross-border diagnostics and international insurance claims expand the digital side of the ecosystem. Global healthcare spending overall reached USD 11 trillion+ in 2025.

    7.5–7.6 Medical Tourism Destinations & Corridors

    DestinationStrengths
    IndiaCost efficiency, specialist doctors, English-speaking ecosystem
    ThailandWellness, elective procedures
    SingaporeHigh-complexity treatments
    UAEPremium healthcare, regional hub
    TurkeyCosmetic surgery, dental tourism
    South KoreaCosmetic surgery, advanced medical technology

    GCC → India is the largest medical payment corridor, followed by Africa → India, Bangladesh → India and Middle East → Turkey.

    7.8–7.10 Insurance, TPAs & Telemedicine

    International health insurance flows through Policyholder → Insurance Company → Hospital → Settlement, with major providers Bupa, Allianz, AXA, Cigna, Aetna and Prudential. Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) — Allianz Partners, MedNet, NextCare — handle claims management and settlement. Telemedicine platforms (Teladoc, Doctor Anywhere, Practo) are a rapidly expanding payment category.

    7.13–7.14 AML & Fraud

    Healthcare payments are generally low-risk but not risk-free: fake medical invoices, insurance fraud, claims inflation and shell clinics are managed through hospital verification, treatment validation and claims monitoring. Fraud typologies include phantom treatments, duplicate claims, upcoding and medical identity theft.

    7.20–7.21 Outlook & Conclusion

    Key investment themes to 2030: medical tourism projected to exceed USD 320 billion, telemedicine ecosystems to surpass USD 500 billion, and cross-border healthcare payment flows overall to exceed USD 600 billion. Healthcare is no longer merely a service industry — it is becoming a global financial ecosystem requiring advanced payment, compliance, treasury and liquidity solutions.

    Sources & Methodology

    Primary: FXC Intelligence, WHO global health expenditure data. Secondary: medical tourism industry association reports.
