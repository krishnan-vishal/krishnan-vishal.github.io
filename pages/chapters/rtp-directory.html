Overview

Real-Time Payments have become the fastest-growing payment infrastructure globally. Industry scale (2025): 100+ RTP networks across 80+ countries, processing an estimated 300–500 billion annual transactions worth USD 300–400 trillion. Forecast (2040): 150+ countries, 1–2 trillion transactions, USD 1–1.5 quadrillion in value.

Cross-border RTP may exceed USD 5–10 trillion annually by 2040. Interoperability models range from Wallet↔RTP and RTP↔RTP to RTP↔QR, RTP↔Stablecoin and RTP↔CBDC — with interoperability becoming more strategically important than network ownership.
