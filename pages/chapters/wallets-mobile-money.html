Executive Summary

Digital wallets have become the fastest-growing payment instrument globally and are increasingly replacing traditional bank accounts as the primary financial interface for consumers. Wallets have evolved from simple payment tools into comprehensive financial ecosystems offering payments, savings, lending, investments, insurance, wealth management, commerce and remittances. More than 4 billion people globally use some form of digital wallet, and the future of cross-border payments is increasingly becoming wallet-to-wallet rather than bank-to-bank.

13.5–13.7 Interoperability, Super Apps & QR

Wallet interoperability is the next major growth driver — leading examples include UPI–PayNow (India–Singapore), PromptPay–UPI (Thailand–India), the Alipay+ Network and BIS Project Nexus. Super apps (WeChat, Alipay, Grab, GoTo, Kakao) combine payments, commerce, lifestyle and financial services in a single application.
