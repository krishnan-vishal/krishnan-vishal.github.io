Global Gaming Payments Industry

Game purchases, in-game virtual currencies, esports prize money and creator revenue sharing form a fast-growing, cross-border financial ecosystem.

Industry Definition

Gaming-related cross-border payments include game purchases, in-game purchases, virtual currency purchases, battle passes, esports prize money, creator revenue sharing, streaming payments and affiliate payments.

In-game virtual currencies (V-Bucks, Robux, Riot Points, UC, Diamonds) process an estimated USD 80–100 billion+ annually.

8.5–8.6 Esports & Streaming Payments

Major esports titles — League of Legends, Valorant, DOTA 2, Counter Strike — generate cross-border prize-pool payouts from sponsorship, advertising, media rights and ticketing. Streaming platforms (Twitch, YouTube Gaming, Kick) distribute ad revenue, donations, sponsorships and subscription payments to creators globally.

8.9 AML & Regulatory Considerations

Key risks include money laundering via virtual assets, account farming and skin trading, and gambling/gaming convergence — managed through KYC, player verification, behavioural analytics and virtual-economy monitoring.

8.14 Chapter Conclusion

Gaming payments have evolved from simple game purchases into a sophisticated cross-border financial ecosystem spanning virtual economies, esports, creator monetisation and emerging digital-asset rails — with the cross-border payment component alone projected to exceed USD 320 billion by 2030.
