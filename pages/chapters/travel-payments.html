    Global Travel, Airline & Hospitality Payments Ecosystem

    Airlines, hotels, OTAs, tourism services and loyalty programs form one of the world's largest cross-border payment ecosystems, worth an estimated USD 4–5 trillion annually.

    Executive Summary

    Travel and tourism represent one of the world's largest cross-border payment ecosystems, encompassing airlines, hotels, online travel agencies, tourism services, car rentals, cruises, visa processing, travel insurance and loyalty programs — a sector highly dependent on cross-border acquiring, FX conversion, card networks and travel wallets.

    Segment2025 Volume
    Airlines$1.0–1.2T
    Hotels & Accommodation$1.2–1.5T
    Tourism Services$700B–1T
    Online Travel Agencies$500–800B
    Travel Insurance$100–150B
    Car Rental & Mobility$200–300B

    Total travel payment ecosystem: USD 4–5 trillion annually.

    22.1–22.2 Tourism Flows & Top Corridors

    Top tourism destinations: France, Spain, USA, Italy, UAE, Thailand, Turkey, Japan, Saudi Arabia and Singapore. Leading corridors: USA → Europe, China → Southeast Asia, GCC → Europe, India → UAE and Europe → USA, with an estimated annual cross-border flow of USD 2–3 trillion.

    22.3–22.5 Airlines, OTAs & Loyalty

    Airline payment flow runs Traveler → Travel Agent/OTA → Airline → Settlement Network, settled through IATA BSP, ARC and UATP, with an estimated USD 800B–1T in annual settlement volume.
