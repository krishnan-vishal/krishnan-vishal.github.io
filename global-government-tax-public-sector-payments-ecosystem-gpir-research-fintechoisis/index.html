Global Government, Tax & Public Sector Payments Ecosystem | GPIR Research | FINTECHOISIS

Governments collectively represent the largest payment ecosystem globally. Every year governments process trillions of dollars across tax collections, social benefits, pension payments, public procurement, salary payments, subsidies, grants, international aid and sovereign transfers &mdash; influencing financial inclusion, economic stability, monetary policy, national security and public welfare. Total government payment ecosystem: USD 50&ndash;80 trillion+ annually.
