Module 01

Executive Overview

Global cross-border payments encompass far more than consumer remittances alone — the true addressable market spans B2B trade settlement, corporate treasury flows, travel and tourism receipts, e-commerce, and cross-border investment activity. Measuring remittances in isolation significantly understates the size and strategic value of this market.

This report classifies the world's most relevant payment markets along two dimensions — outward (sending) capability and inward (receiving) capability — and estimates each country's total annual cross-border payment volume. The classification draws on industry benchmarks published by SWIFT, the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), the World Bank, the IMF, the ECB, the WTO, McKinsey, and FXC Intelligence.

Module 06

Key Takeaways

  • The United States and China anchor global cross-border flows, together representing well over half of total estimated volume.

  • Treasury and liquidity hubs (US, UK, Singapore, UAE, Hong Kong, Switzerland) punch far above their economic size due to correspondent banking and FX concentration.

  • High-remittance corridors (India, Philippines, Pakistan, Nigeria, Mexico) represent the largest opportunity for consumer-facing and wallet-based payment innovation.

  • Emerging corridors across Southeast Asia, Africa, and the GCC offer the strongest long-term growth trajectory as digital payment infrastructure matures.
