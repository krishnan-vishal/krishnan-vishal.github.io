Global Gig Economy, Freelancer & Creator Payments Industry | GPIR Research | FINTECHOISIS

The gig economy, creator economy, freelance workforce and borderless employment ecosystem has emerged as one of the fastest-growing segments within global cross-border payments. A new workforce model &mdash; freelancers, independent contractors, digital nomads, remote employees, content creators, influencers and AI service providers &mdash; generates recurring cross-border payout flows increasingly rivalling traditional remittance volumes in certain corridors.
