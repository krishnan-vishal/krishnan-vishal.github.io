Global Legalized Betting, Gambling, Lottery & Wagering Payments | GPIR Research | FINTECHOISIS

The legalised betting and gambling industry represents one of the most lucrative yet heavily regulated payment ecosystems globally. Betting transactions involve high transaction velocity, large deposit volumes, frequent withdrawals, elevated AML risks and enhanced regulatory oversight. The ecosystem spans sports betting, online casinos, poker, fantasy sports, lottery and esports betting, with payment activity flowing Player &rarr; Operator &rarr; Payment Provider &rarr; Acquirer &rarr; Regulator &rarr; Tax Authority.
