Executive intelligence

GPIR preserves 218 dated CNY/INR reference observations from 2 January 2026 through 6 November 2026. Within that verified window, stored rates ranged from 12.852237 to 14.454408 INR per CNY; the first observation was 12.896807 and the latest was 14.146903. The archive lineage combines Frankfurter's ECB-backed dated series with the existing reference-open-er-api capture path. Stored methods for this pair are derived-cross. Cross rates are derived only from same-date source observations. These values are reference observations, not executable quotes, remittance prices or recommendations. The evidence does not establish a market cause, policy effect or forecast; execution time, spreads, fees, liquidity and settlement terms are outside this publication.
