14.3&ndash;14.6 SWIFT, Correspondent Banking & RTP Networks

Founded in 1973, SWIFT reaches 200+ countries, 11,000+ institutions and processes 50+ million daily messages &mdash; still the dominant international banking network, modernising through SWIFT GPI and ISO 20022 migration. Correspondent banking remains the historical backbone (Bank A &rarr; Correspondent &rarr; Correspondent &rarr; Bank B), with a declining share but continued importance for large-value payments.

14.7&ndash;14.9 Project Nexus, Orchestration & ISO 20022

Project Nexus, sponsored by the Bank for International Settlements, aims to connect existing RTP systems globally rather than creating a new network &mdash; a potential "SWIFT for RTP Networks." Payment orchestration ("connect once, access many rails") is the future competitive battleground, led by Nium, Thunes, TerraPay, Banking Circle and Airwallex. ISO 20022 is the future global messaging standard, delivering rich data, better compliance and improved reconciliation.

Chapter Conclusion

The cross-border payments value chain spans six interlocking participant layers, from balance-sheet-holding banks at the core through money movement specialists to consumer- and merchant-facing wallets and platforms at the edge. No single rail will dominate globally &mdash; the future is multi-rail, multi-currency, multi-network, AI-orchestrated infrastructure.
