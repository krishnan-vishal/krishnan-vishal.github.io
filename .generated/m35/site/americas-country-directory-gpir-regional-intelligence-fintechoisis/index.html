Americas Regional Intelligence

GPIR intends to build a regional Americas view covering cross-border P2P volume, diaspora and migration concentration, corridor economics (e.g. US&ndash;Mexico, US&ndash;Canada, US&ndash;Brazil, US&ndash;Dominican Republic, Spain&ndash;LATAM), the fintech ecosystem, payment infrastructure and regulatory developments across the whole hemisphere &mdash; spanning both the 14 countries above and the LATAM-canonical markets they connect to, since corridors like US&ndash;Mexico don't respect where a country's intelligence record happens to live. None of that regional-level data has been compiled or validated yet, so it is listed here as roadmap topics rather than populated with figures. Corridor significance will be driven by validated data, not hard-coded assumptions, once sourced:

GPIR will not aggregate individual country figures into a regional total unless the underlying methodology genuinely supports it &mdash; a regional number will not be published as a shortcut once countries are researched independently.

This directory reflects the GPIR Americas roadmap honestly: no Americas country has a published Country Intelligence page yet, so every entry above is shown as Coming Soon. Countries are grouped by Northern America, Central America, Caribbean and South America for navigation only, following the UN M49 geographic classification &mdash; this does not imply that payment ecosystems, regulation or economic characteristics are identical within a grouping. The 21 countries that are already canonical LATAM markets (Mexico and the rest of Central America, Cuba, the Dominican Republic, Haiti, and all ten South American LATAM markets) are deliberately not listed here a second time &mdash; each sub-region they fully or partly cover links straight to the LATAM directory instead. Only the 14 countries with no LATAM equivalent (Canada, United States, ten sovereign Caribbean states, Guyana and Suriname) appear as Americas country tiles. As each is researched and published, it will move from Coming Soon to Active using the same reusable Country Intelligence template built for APAC, LATAM and Europe/SEPA &mdash; so Americas never needs a second, conflicting set of country pages.
