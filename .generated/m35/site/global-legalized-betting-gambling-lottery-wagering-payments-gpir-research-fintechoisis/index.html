Module 01

Executive Summary

The legalised betting and gambling industry represents one of the most lucrative yet heavily regulated payment ecosystems globally. Betting transactions involve high transaction velocity, large deposit volumes, frequent withdrawals, elevated AML risks and enhanced regulatory oversight. The ecosystem spans sports betting, online casinos, poker, fantasy sports, lottery and esports betting, with payment activity flowing Player → Operator → Payment Provider → Acquirer → Regulator → Tax Authority.

8—9.10 AML, KYC & Regulation

One of the strictest AML sectors globally: structuring, chip dumping, layering, smurfing and bonus abuse are managed through identity verification, address verification, source-of-funds/wealth checks and enhanced due diligence for VIP players, PEPs and sanctioned persons.

Module 09

13 Chapter Conclusion

Legalised betting, gambling, lottery and wagering payments remain among the most lucrative yet operationally demanding payment ecosystems globally — requiring sophisticated treasury, AML and regulatory infrastructure across a market projected to exceed USD 1.1 trillion by 2030, with cross-border payment flows alone reaching USD 450–550 billion.
