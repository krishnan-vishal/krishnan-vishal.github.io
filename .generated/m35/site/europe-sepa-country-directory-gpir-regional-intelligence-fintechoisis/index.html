Euro Area

EU member states that have adopted the euro as their currency and are formal Euro Area (ECB monetary union) members.

EU — Non-Euro

EU member states that retain their own national currency outside the Euro Area.

EEA / Non-EU

European Economic Area members that are not EU member states.

Non-EEA SEPA

SEPA geographical-scope participants that are neither EU nor EEA members.

United Kingdom

A separate, highly visible SEPA participant — not EU, not EEA, not Euro Area.

SEPA Regional Intelligence

GPIR intends to build a regional Europe/SEPA view covering SEPA payment volumes (SCT, SCT Inst, SDD Core, SDD B2B), cross-border P2P remittances, diaspora and migration concentration, corridor economics, the fintech ecosystem, Open Banking interoperability, and regulatory developments across all 41 markets above &mdash; the same intelligence layers already scoped for each individual country, plus the SEPA-specific payment-scheme layer that sits alongside them. None of that regional-level data has been compiled or validated yet, so it is listed here as roadmap topics rather than populated with figures:

GPIR will not aggregate individual country figures into a regional total unless the underlying methodology genuinely supports it &mdash; a regional number will not be published as a shortcut once countries are researched independently.

This directory reflects the GPIR Europe/SEPA roadmap honestly: the United Kingdom has a published Country Intelligence page; the other 40 SEPA markets are shown as Coming Soon because no page has been published for them yet. Countries are grouped by Euro Area, EU &mdash; Non-Euro, EEA / Non-EU, Non-EEA SEPA and United Kingdom for navigation and classification clarity &mdash; SEPA geographical scope, EU membership, EEA membership and Euro Area membership are tracked as four independent fields per country, never conflated into one label. Every classification chip and currency shown is sourced from the European Payments Council&rsquo;s current SEPA geographical scope; GPIR has not independently verified per-institution scheme adherence or PSP operational readiness for any market. As countries are researched and published, each will move from Coming Soon to Active using the same reusable Country Intelligence template built for APAC (India, Singapore) and GCC (UAE), extended with a SEPA Payment Intelligence section &mdash; so Europe never needs 41 separate page designs.
