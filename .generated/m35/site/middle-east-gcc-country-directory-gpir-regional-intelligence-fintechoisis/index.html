Middle East / GCC Regional Intelligence

GPIR intends to build a regional Middle East / GCC view covering GCC remittance outflows, cross-border P2P volume, diaspora and migrant-worker concentration, exchange house / MTO participation, the FinTech ecosystem, digital banking, open banking, payment infrastructure, GCC-specific corridor economics (for example UAE&ndash;India, UAE&ndash;Pakistan, Saudi Arabia&ndash;India, Qatar&ndash;India), and regulatory developments across all 13 markets above &mdash; the same intelligence layers already scoped for each individual country, extended with the Exchange House / MTO Intelligence layer that is particularly relevant to GCC markets. None of that regional-level data has been compiled or validated yet, so it is listed here as roadmap topics rather than populated with figures. Corridor significance will be driven by validated data, not hard-coded assumptions, once sourced:

GPIR will not aggregate individual country figures into a regional total unless the underlying methodology genuinely supports it &mdash; a regional number will not be published as a shortcut once countries are researched independently.

This directory reflects the GPIR Middle East / GCC roadmap honestly. United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia and Qatar already have real, published GPIR intelligence &mdash; a Country Intelligence page for UAE and homepage Dashboard Gallery entries for Saudi Arabia and Qatar &mdash; and every tile above for those three links straight through to that existing record rather than duplicating it. The other ten markets (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria, Iraq, Iran, Yemen) are new canonical records shown honestly as Coming Soon, with real flag and currency data but no fabricated content. Countries are grouped by GCC, Levant, Mesopotamia, Iran/Persian Gulf and Arabian Peninsula for navigation only &mdash; this does not imply that payment ecosystems, regulation, currencies or economic conditions are identical within a grouping, and Iran's inclusion under its own heading does not imply GCC membership. Palestine's currency line intentionally lists ILS / JOD / USD rather than a single fabricated ISO 4217 code, reflecting its actual multi-currency circulation. As each new country is researched and published, it will move from Coming Soon to Active using the same reusable Country Intelligence template built for APAC, LATAM, Europe/SEPA, the Americas and Africa &mdash; extended with the Exchange House / MTO Intelligence layer given its structural importance across GCC markets &mdash; so the Middle East never needs 13 separate page designs.
