Foundations

Foundations should make the remainder of the repository understandable without turning the landing page into a textbook. The module therefore preserves the research's definition, reserve mechanics, stablecoin categories, distinction from volatile crypto assets, payment relevance and core use cases, while compressing repeated explanations. 2.2 Refined Foundations Narrative — Publication Draft 2.3 Foundation Component A — What Is a Stablecoin? 2.4 Foundation Component B — Reserve & Redemption Mechanics 2.5 Foundation Component C — Stablecoin Types 2.6 Foundation Component D — Stablecoin vs Volatile Crypto 2.7 Foundation Component E — Payment Use Cases 2.8 Foundation Key Takeaways — Refined • Stablecoins should be analysed as settlement instruments and payment infrastructure, not simply as another cryptocurrency category. • Fiat-backed instruments are the central focus for regulated cross-border payment use because reserve quality, redemption and compliance acceptance are operationally critical. • Fast on-chain transfer does not equal a complete cross-border payment; on-ramp, FX, liquidity, off-ramp and local payout remain part of the journey. • Different stablecoin structures carry materially different risks and should not be grouped into one undifferentiated market category. • The correct strategic test is end-to-end corridor performance: cost, speed, liquidity, regulation, consumer/business protection and operational resilience. 2.9 Recommended Web Layout Review Gate — Modules 01 & 02 • Approve / amend the density of the Executive Intelligence narrative. • Approve / amend the seven critical findings and their ordering. • Approve the Foundations compression model: cards + smart-shape flow + expandable evidence. • Confirm whether the C2C use-case card remains visible in Foundations even though the supplied executive dashboard is explicitly non-C2C. • Approve the three evidence labels: SOURCE-VALIDATED | GPIR ANALYSIS | OUTLOOK / SCENARIO.
