Executive Summary

Travel and tourism represent one of the world's largest cross-border payment ecosystems, encompassing airlines, hotels, online travel agencies, tourism services, car rentals, cruises, visa processing, travel insurance and loyalty programs &mdash; a sector highly dependent on cross-border acquiring, FX conversion, card networks and travel wallets.

Total travel payment ecosystem: USD 4&ndash;5 trillion annually.

22.1&ndash;22.2 Tourism Flows & Top Corridors

Top tourism destinations: France, Spain, USA, Italy, UAE, Thailand, Turkey, Japan, Saudi Arabia and Singapore. Leading corridors: USA &rarr; Europe, China &rarr; Southeast Asia, GCC &rarr; Europe, India &rarr; UAE and Europe &rarr; USA, with an estimated annual cross-border flow of USD 2&ndash;3 trillion.

22.3&ndash;22.5 Airlines, OTAs & Loyalty

Airline payment flow runs Traveler &rarr; Travel Agent/OTA &rarr; Airline &rarr; Settlement Network, settled through IATA BSP, ARC and UATP, with an estimated USD 800B&ndash;1T in annual settlement volume.
