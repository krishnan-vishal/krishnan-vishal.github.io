Executive Summary

Digital wallets have become the fastest-growing payment instrument globally and are increasingly replacing traditional bank accounts as the primary financial interface for consumers. Wallets have evolved from simple payment tools into comprehensive financial ecosystems offering payments, savings, lending, investments, insurance, wealth management, commerce and remittances. More than 4 billion people globally use some form of digital wallet, and the future of cross-border payments is increasingly becoming wallet-to-wallet rather than bank-to-bank.

13.3&ndash;13.4 Regional Leaders & Mobile Money

Mobile money reaches an estimated 2 billion+ active accounts, processing USD 1.5&ndash;2 trillion annually in 2025, forecast to reach USD 4&ndash;5 trillion by 2030.

13.5&ndash;13.7 Interoperability, Super Apps & QR

Wallet interoperability is the next major growth driver &mdash; leading examples include UPI&ndash;PayNow (India&ndash;Singapore), PromptPay&ndash;UPI (Thailand&ndash;India), the Alipay+ Network and BIS Project Nexus. Super apps (WeChat, Alipay, Grab, GoTo, Kakao) combine payments, commerce, lifestyle and financial services in a single application.

13.10 Wallet Business Models

Payments increasingly become customer-acquisition tools rather than the primary revenue source &mdash; financial services increasingly drive wallet profitability.

13.14&ndash;13.15 2040 Forecast & Conclusion

By 2040, wallets are likely to become the primary gateway through which billions of people access payments, commerce, savings, investments, insurance, credit, government services and cross-border financial services.
