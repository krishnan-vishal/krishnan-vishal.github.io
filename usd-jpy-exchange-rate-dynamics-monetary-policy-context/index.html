USD/JPY — Exchange Rate Dynamics and Monetary Policy Context

USD/JPY exchange-rate dynamics, monetary-policy context, cross-border payment implications and research limitations.

Executive intelligence

USD/JPY is a material global FX pair: the BIS reported a 14.3% share of global OTC FX turnover in April 2025. GPIR's 2 January–4 November 2026 reference history uses same-date ECB USD and JPY observations to derive the cross. October 2026 policy statements from the Bank of Japan and Federal Reserve provide verified monetary-policy context, but they do not by themselves prove why the pair moved. Payment and treasury readers should treat the GPIR series as reference evidence, not an executable quote or forecast.

Module 01

Scope and reference period

This publication concerns USD/JPY for the reference period 2 January through 4 November 2026. GPIR's FX data authority records 216 same-date fixing observations for the period. The pair is derived as a cross from official ECB EUR reference-rate observations for USD and JPY; it is not an executable market quote, remittance rate or provider price.

Module 02

Structural market context

The BIS Triennial Central Bank Survey reports that USD/JPY represented 14.3% of global OTC foreign-exchange turnover in April 2025 on a net-net basis, compared with 13.5% in 2022. This is a structural market-activity measure for April 2025, not evidence of the pair's direction in 2026.

Module 03

Verified monetary-policy context

On 30 October 2026, the Bank of Japan stated that it would encourage the uncollateralized overnight call rate to remain at around 1.25% for the intermeeting period. On 28 October 2026, the Federal Open Market Committee maintained the federal funds target range at 3.75%–4.00%. These official settings establish contemporaneous policy context for the two currencies.

Module 04

Correlation and causality boundary

Interest-rate settings and expectations are relevant to currency valuation, but this draft does not attribute any observed USD/JPY move to either central bank. Establishing causality would require a defined event study, intraday market data, controls for simultaneous information and evidence about market expectations. The daily reference series alone cannot provide that proof.

Module 05

Cross-border payment and treasury implications

Variation in USD/JPY changes the reference conversion between dollar- and yen-denominated obligations. The BIS identifies outright forwards as instruments used to lock future exchange rates and FX swaps as instruments used to manage FX funding liquidity and hedge currency risk.

This publication does not recommend a product or strategy. Actual payment and treasury outcomes depend on execution time, spreads, fees, liquidity, settlement arrangements and provider terms that are not represented by the ECB reference series.

Methodology: GPIR preserves source fixing dates and derives non-EUR crosses only from same-date ECB observations. Editorial claims are limited to facts supported by the cited primary sources.

Limitations: Daily reference rates do not capture intraday reactions, market expectations, executable prices, transaction costs or causal effects. No forecast is provided.
